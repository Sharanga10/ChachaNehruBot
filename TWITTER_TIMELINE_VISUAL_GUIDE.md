## 📊 **ENGAGEMENT METRICS**

Each tweet shows **real-time engagement metrics**:

| Metric | Icon | Description |
|--------|------|-------------|
| **Replies** | 💬 | Number of replies to the tweet |
| **Retweets** | 🔄 | Number of retweets/shares |
| **Likes** | ❤️ | Number of likes/hearts |
| **Views** | 📊 | Total impressions/views |
| **Quality** | ⭐ | AI-calculated quality score (1-10) |

### **Quality Score Colors**
- **🟢 Green (8.0-10.0)**: Excellent quality
- **🟡 Yellow (6.0-7.9)**: Good quality  
- **🔴 Red (0.0-5.9)**: Needs improvement

## 🔄 **REAL-TIME UPDATES**

### **Auto-Refresh Feature**
- **✅ Enabled by default** - Timeline updates every 30 seconds
- **🔄 Manual refresh** - Click "🔄 Refresh Timeline" anytime
- **📱 Live notifications** - Shows "🔄 Refreshing timeline..." during updates
- **✅ Success feedback** - "✅ Timeline updated with latest tweets!"

### **New Tweet Animation**
When the bot posts a new tweet:
1. **🎯 Activity log** shows "Generating content..."
2. **✅ Success message** appears in log
3. **🐦 Timeline tab** badge shows update indicator
4. **📱 New tweet** appears at top of timeline
5. **🎉 Optional notification** popup
